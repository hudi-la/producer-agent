隐私说明 · Privacy · 2026-10-04

我们存什么

  • 你用 Google 登录时,我们只拿你的邮箱、名字和头像,用来认出你。
  • 你在 Fairwind 里写的档案、作品、机会卡、判据和上传的文件,存在数据库里,只有你的账号能读写。
  • 你填的模型 API key 或订阅令牌,只用来替你调用你选的模型服务,不会给任何别人。
  • 如果你在设置里连接 Gmail:系统存一把只属于你的授权钥匙,每 5 分钟替你收一次信。所有信只存信头(发件人、收件人、标题、时间);只有跟你机会卡有关的那些信才存正文,用来写摘要、判断下一步。发信只在你在页面上按「发送」时发生,模型自己发不了信;按「放进草稿箱」时会在你的 Gmail 里建一封草稿,不会发出。在设置里按「断开」,钥匙当场删除,系统不再收你的信;已经收进来的记录留在你的账号里,要删照下面「删除」那条。

我们不做什么

  • 不卖、不分享你的数据给第三方。
  • 不连 Gmail 就不碰你的信箱;连了也只用来做上面写的这些,不拿你的邮件做广告、不卖、不给别人,也不拿去训练模型。
  • 模型调用费用走你自己的 key,我们不经手。

错误诊断日志

发生错误时，我们会记录错误编号、接收时间、操作类别、错误类型、HTTP 状态和版本标记，用于排查故障。这些诊断记录不包含姓名、邮箱、密码、API key、令牌、对话正文或文件内容，也不发送到第三方分析服务。记录仅供服务器管理员查看，最多保留 14 天，总大小约 5 MB；超过容量时会提前清理旧记录。断网时，浏览器标签页会暂存最多 50 条诊断记录，联网后补传，超过 24 小时丢弃。

删除

设置页里可以下载你的全部数据。要删账号和所有数据,写信给 hudi.creates@gmail.com。

English

We store your Google email, name and avatar to identify you, and the content you create in Fairwind, readable only by your account. API keys you enter are used only to call the model service you chose. If you connect Gmail in Settings, we store an authorization token that belongs only to you and fetch your mail every 5 minutes. We store headers (sender, recipients, subject, time) for all messages, and the body only for messages related to your opportunity cards, which is used to summarize them and suggest next steps. Mail is only sent when you press Send on the page; the model cannot send mail on its own. Pressing "Put in drafts" creates a draft in your Gmail without sending it. Disconnecting in Settings deletes the token immediately. Gmail data is used only to provide these features: it is never used for advertising, sold, shared, or used to train models. We do not sell or share your data. To export your data use Settings; to delete your account email hudi.creates@gmail.com.

Error diagnostics contain only an error reference, receipt time, operation category, error category, HTTP status and release marker. They contain no names, email addresses, credentials, conversation text or file content, and are not sent to third-party analytics. Server administrators can access them for up to 14 days, with a total size limit of approximately 5 MB (older entries may be removed sooner). During connection failures, the browser tab queues up to 50 diagnostic records for up to 24 hours.
