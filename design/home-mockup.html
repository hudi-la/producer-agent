项目陈述 · 开头EN 草稿

My installations begin from a physicist's habit: to take an invisible structure and make it something you can stand inside. In Echo Chamber Realms, I let multi-agent AI systems talk until patterns of attachment, competition, and care surface on their own — then I stage the encounter so a visitor doesn't just watch the pattern, but meets it in themselves.

At Eyebeam I want to push this from a scene one witnesses toward one the visitor co-authors…

我把"物理学家→做装置"的转折放最前面——对 art-tech 评审这是你最强的钩子，也是真的你。语气压得克制，他们要方法和问题，不要自谦。这个开头对味吗？
