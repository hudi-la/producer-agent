# Fairwind

**A personal producer workspace for an artist’s opportunities, applications, and professional relationships.**

### [Visit Fairwind →](https://fairwind.hudiworks.com/)

Created by [Hu Di](https://hudiworks.com/).

## Keeping an artistic practice moving

An opportunity rarely ends with finding a call or sending a proposal. There are works to select, materials to prepare, people to remember, replies to consider, and conversations to return to months later. Fairwind brings that ongoing work into one place.

Built from the needs of an independent artistic practice, it connects opportunity research and AI-assisted drafting with a persistent record of projects, materials, correspondence, and next steps.

## In the workspace

- **Opportunity pipeline:** follow residencies, exhibitions, grants, workshops, and other possibilities from discovery through preparation, submission, and further conversation.
- **An artist’s archive:** keep works, statements, experience, and supporting materials available for each application.
- **Research and drafting:** develop briefs and application materials in the context of a specific opportunity.
- **Professional relationships:** keep contacts, correspondence, and meeting notes connected to the projects they concern.
- **Follow-up and continuity:** see what needs attention and return to the history of a conversation when deciding the next step.

The aim is to make the administrative and relational work around an artistic practice easier to carry forward over time.
